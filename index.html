Credit Card Fraud Detection

Train a logistic regression model from Kaggle transaction data, evaluate its results, and upload transactions for fraud predictions.

The common Kaggle credit-card dataset uses Class as its label: 0 means legitimate and 1 means fraud. Training uses numeric features, scales them, and balances the classes.
